08 / A few questionsBefore you write

Good to know.

Do I need strategy experience?

You can get to know MSC at any level. Strategy Summit welcomes students from all programs; bring curiosity and a willingness to work through a challenge.

How do I join?

Use “Join MSC” to write an introduction. Follow the delivery instructions shown with your letter; the council can then explain current membership steps.

When is Strategy Summit?

Registration closes October 26 at noon. Follow @msc.uoft for the event schedule and registration link. Teams have 4–5 students, and all programs are welcome.

Where are announcements?

Follow @msc.uoft on Instagram ↗ for confirmed events, Strategy Summit and council news.

Are executive applications open?

No application deadline is listed here yet. Check @msc.uoft for current openings, or mention your interest in a letter.

Who should partners contact?

Anudari Esen, Jayden Lee and Angelie Samlal in Corporate Relations. Use “Partner with MSC” to introduce your organization and idea.
