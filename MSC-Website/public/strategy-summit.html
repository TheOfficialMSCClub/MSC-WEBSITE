There’s an opportunity in the everyday.

See it differently.

Turn an everyday observation into a product worth building. A student-led case competition at the intersection of technology, strategy and entrepreneurship.

This year, we’re bringing students into the technology, strategy and start-up space. Research a real-world scenario, gather evidence, and turn your observations into a recommendation worth presenting.

University of Toronto tri-campus, with UTSC at the heart. Every degree and major is welcome, with one shared competition division.
